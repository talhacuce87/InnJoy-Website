InnJoy

Security & Integrations

InnJoy provides a reliable foundation for modern hotel operations. Authentication, data storage and access control are designed with privacy principles in mind.

Infrastructure

Secure sign‑in with Firebase Authentication; low‑latency, scalable data with Cloud Firestore.

Role‑Based Access

Authorization based on Admin/Guest roles. Admin screens are accessible only to authorized users; audit trails are preserved.

Data Privacy

Personal data is used only for operational necessity; deletion and anonymization policies are supported.

Emergency Flow

Guests send an Alert with a single tap; relevant staff are notified immediately with room and location context.

Designed to provide reassurance and a rapid response.

Integrations

Integrate with PMS and payment providers over REST/GraphQL. Sync data with event management, room service and billing flows.
