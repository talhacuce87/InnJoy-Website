As InnJoy Teknoloji, we have prepared this clarification text to inform you about the processing of your personal data as Data Controller in accordance with the Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 ("KVKK").

1. Processed Personal Data

  • Identity Information: Name, Surname, TR ID No/Passport No (May be mandatory during reservation).
  • Contact Information: Phone number, e-mail address, notification address.
  • Account Information: Username, password (irreversibly encrypted), profile information, membership date.
  • Location Information: Only if you give explicit consent (to show nearby hotels).
  • Financial Data: Payment method type (Card information is processed directly by the Payment Institution as per PCI DSS standards and is not stored on Application servers).
  • Transaction Security: Device information (ID), IP address, log records, session information.
  • Usage Data: In-app movements, reservation history, favorites, search preferences.
  • Visual Data: Profile Picture/Photo (Only if uploaded by the user).

2. Purposes of Processing Personal Data

  1. Creating membership registration, managing your account, and providing identity verification transactions.
  2. Carrying out hotel selection, reservation, payment, and service usage processes completely and correctly (Establishment and Performance of the Contract).
  3. Increasing the performance of the application, error analyzes, and improving the user experience (Legitimate Interest).
  4. Managing customer services and support requests, resolving complaints.
  5. Fulfilling obligations arising from legal regulations and requests from administrative authorities (Legal Obligation).
  6. Carrying out campaigns, notifications, and marketing activities and offering personalized offers if you give explicit consent.

3. Parties to Whom Personal Data is Transferred

  • Service Providing Hotel Businesses: Identity and reservation details mandatory for the performance of the reservation.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Companies from which we receive cloud storage, server, analytics, and technical infrastructure services (e.g., Firebase, hosting companies).
  • Authorized Public Institutions and Organizations: Administrative and judicial authorities legally authorized to fulfill obligations arising from laws.
  • Payment Institutions: Relevant financial institutions for the secure completion of payment transactions.

4. Method and Legal Reason for Collecting Personal Data

Your personal data is collected by automatic methods in the electronic environment, such as filling out the membership form via the mobile application, making a reservation, submitting a support request via e-mail, or logging in via social media.

The legal reasons for collection activities are as follows:

  • KVKK Art. 5/2-c: Being directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract (e.g., Reservation transactions).
  • KVKK Art. 5/2-ç: Fulfillment of legal obligation (e.g., Tax, invoice records).
  • KVKK Art. 5/2-f: Data processing being mandatory for the legitimate interest of the data controller, provided that it does not harm the fundamental rights and freedoms of the person concerned (e.g., Security of the application and performance analysis).
  • KVKK Art. 5/1: Explicit consent (e.g., Marketing communication, personalization, location information permission).

5. Storage Period and Deletion of Personal Data

Your personal data is stored during legal obligations (Tax Law, Commercial Law, etc.) and the continuation of the service relationship.

  • In case of termination of your membership, your data may continue to be stored for the period required by the legislation throughout the legal statute of limitations (usually 10 years).
  • At the end of this period or if there is no legal obstacle, your data is deleted, destroyed, or anonymized in accordance with the legislation.

6. Your Rights Under KVKK

By applying to us in accordance with Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to;

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • Request information if it has been processed,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in accordance with its purpose,
  • Know the third parties to whom it is transferred domestically or abroad,
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or incorrectly processed,
  • Request deletion or destruction under KVKK conditions,
  • Object to the emergence of a result against you by analyzing the processed data exclusively through automated systems,
  • Withdraw your explicit consent.

You can submit your requests in writing or via registered electronic mail (KEP) address, secure electronic signature, mobile signature, or via your e-mail address previously notified to us and registered in our system:
E-mail: support@innjoy.com
